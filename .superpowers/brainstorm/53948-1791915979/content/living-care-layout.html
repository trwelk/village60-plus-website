Plain explanations of independent living, residential care, respite, recovery and rehabilitation, and how the team helps you choose.

Two kinds of stay.

01 A home 02 A while

01

A home

A longer stay. A room that becomes yours, with support nearby.

Available

Residential Care

A comfortable home with a care team on hand around the clock for personal support, meals and daily routines.

Read about residential care
Limited

Independent Living

A private home of your own with shared spaces, everyday services and support quietly available when you want it.

Read about independent living

Before you choose

You do not need to know which type before you call.

Describe the situation. The team will explain the options in plain language. This is not a medical assessment.

02

A while

A stay with an end date. The length is the difference.

One to four weeks

Respite Care

A short, planned stay that gives a family carer a break while the person they care for is looked after.

Two to six weeks

Recovery

Supported time to regain strength after a hospital stay, illness or surgery before returning home.

Agreed with your medical team

Rehabilitation

A structured programme of therapy and support, coordinated with your own medical team, to rebuild confidence and mobility.
